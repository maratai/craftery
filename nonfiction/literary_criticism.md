# Literary Criticism
Also related genres.

- Jean Baudrillard. *Simulacra and Simulation*. Trans. Sheila Faria Glaser.
- Carl Freedman. *Critical Theory and Science Fiction*. Includes discussions of Stanislaw Lem's *Solaris*, Le Guin's *The Dispossessed*, Joanna Russ's *The Two of Them*, Samuel Delany's *Stars in My Pocket Like Grains of Sand*, and Philip K. Dick's *The Man in the High Castle*.
- Stephanie Kent and Logan Smalley. *The Call Me Ishmael Phone Book: An Interactive Guide to Life-Changing Books*. This isn't literary criticism as such, but it is a delightful directory of testimonials. As of 2025 when I picked it up, the phone-based system was still working.
