# Figure Drawing
- Rockhe Kim. *Rockhe Kim's Anatomy Drawing Class*. Looks excellent although I've only worked through a tiny portion. IIRC Kim's books are originally in Korean but also available in English translation.
- Rockhe Kim. *Rockhe Kim's Line Drawing Technique vol. 1*. Oriented toward comic (etc) lineart for figures.
- Taco. (Apparently the artist-writer's handle, Korean?) *Point Character Drawing 1* and *Point Character Drawing 2*. Pocket-sized and excellent quick reference.
