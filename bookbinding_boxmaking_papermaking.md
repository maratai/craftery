# Bookbinding, Box-Making, and Similar
- Valeria Ferrari and Ersilia Fiorucci. *Paper Crafting Beautiful Book Covers, Boxes and Frames*. Appears to draw on Italian? traditional techniques. **to-read**
- Tom Hollander and Cindy Hollaner. *Constructing and Covering Boxes*, 2nd ed. and *Introduction to Bookbinding and Custom Cases*. Both books are absolutely terrific. **Recommended**.
- Keith A. Smith. *Non-Adhesive Binding: Books without Paste or Glue, Volume I*. Amazing. Thanks to Stephanie Folse. **Recommended**.

# Paper Engineering and Pop-Ups
- David A. Carter and James Diaz. *The Elements of Pop-Up*. The mechanisms are exposed for your examination; the entire book contains working examples. **Recommended**.

# Papermaking
- Dard Hunter. *Papermaking: The History and Technique of an Ancient Craft*.
