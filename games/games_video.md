# CRPGs (computer roleplaying games)
- Felipe Pepe, ed. *The CRPG Book: A Guide to Computer Role-Playing Games, Expanded Edition*. Discussions from 1975 to 2019. Delightful to browse.

# Video Game Development (General)
- [Game Devs of Color Expo](https://www.gamedevsofcolor.org). Highly recommended; many past talks are available as recordings. I sat in on some of the 2026 talks. You don't have to be a game dev or a person of color to listen in/learn or show support! Hat-tip to Anni Movsisyan.
