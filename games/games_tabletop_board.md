Excludes TTRPGs (Tabletop RPGs) not because I don't like them but because they deserve a file of their own! Includes books that are *about* a specific game (e.g. Go manuals).

# Card Games
- Matthew Lowes. *Dungeon Solitaire: Labyrinth of Souls*. Solo game. Addictive and terrific. The "introductory," simpler version, [Dungeon Solitaire: Tomb of Four Kings](https://matthewlowes.com/games/) runs off a regular deck of playing cards and has free rules (PDF download) in English, Spanish, and Korean. **Recommended**.

# Go (Baduk, Wei Qi)
- Daniele Pecorini and Tong Shu. *The Game of Wei-Chi*.
- Arthur Smith. *The Game of Go: The National Game of Japan*.
- Kano Yoshinori 9-dan. *Graded Go Problems for Beginners, 30-kyu to 25-kyu*, Volume One.

# Puzzle Games
- Tom Brinton. *Paper Apps: Galaxy*. Extremely cute solo survival/tactical puzzle game with roguelike vibes. There's a print and play version too.
