# Beading and Beadweaving
- Laura McCabe. *Laura McCabe's Embellished Beadweaving: Jewelry Lavished with Fringe, Fronds, Lacework and More*. Library book.

# Resin Jewelry
- Gay Isber. *Learn to Make Amazing Resin and Epoxy Clay Jewelry: Basic Step-by-Step Projects for Beginners*.
- Sara Naumann. *How to Make Resin Jewellery*.

# Wire-Wrapped Jewelry
- Abby Hook. *Wire Jewelry Masterclass*. This starts with simple projects and goes to advanced ones. I suppose it's fortunate that wire-weaving leaves me cold aesthetically or I would be pining after a very spendy hobby! Sumptuous instructions and projects. **Recommended**.
- Jim McIntosh. *Wire Wrapping: Simple Techniques for Beautiful Designs*. Unprepossessing yet an excellent, practical starting point for wire-wrapping techniques. **Recommended**.
