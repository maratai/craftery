I've separated this out from art instruction since it's sufficiently specialized and technical. My particular interest is raster frame-by-frame 2D animation, so I tend to ignore materials *specific* to 3D animation, bones or inverse kinematics rigging, stop-motion etc. They're perfectly valid artistically and I've seen terrific examples of all of these, but I decided to pick a lane.

# Acting and Body Language
Some of these resources will be a bit out of field.

- Allan and Barbara Pease. *The Definitive Book of Body Language*.

# Animation Principles and Theory
- Ellen Besen, illus. Bryce Hallett. *Animation Unleashed*. Terrific overview; the illustrations are integral to the text. **Recommended**.
- Preston Blair. *Advanced Animation*.
- Richard Williams. *The Animator's Survival Kit*, expanded edition.

# Animation Timing
- Harold Whitaker and John Halas. Updated by Tom Sito. *Timing for Animation*, 3rd ed. 2021. **to-read**
