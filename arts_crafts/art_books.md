# Art Books
- David Mack. *Reflections: A Book of Paintings and Drawings*. **Recommended**.
- Stephanie Law. *Metamorph: The Art of Stephanie Law: June 10 - August 6, 2017*. Krab Jab Studio. Exquisite. **Recommended**.

# Chinese Art
- W. M. Hawley. *Chinese Folk Designs: A Collection of 300 Cut-Paper Designs*. **Recommended**.
- C. A. S. Williams. *Outlines of Chinese Symbolism and Art Motives*, 3rd revised ed.

# Fashion
- Hannah Kane. *The Style Thesaurus*.

# Japanese Art and Architecture
- *Kikko Toshogu Shrine*. Acquired secondhand; apparently a souvenir book, with luxurious full-color photographs. **Recommended**.
- Matsuya Piece-Goods Store. *Japanese Design Motifs: 4,260 Illustrations of Japanese Crests*. Trans. Fumie Adachi. Black-and-white drawings of *mon* (?) with great clarity and detail. **Recommended**.
