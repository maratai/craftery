# Personal Fiber Hierarchy
This is for me regarding ease and pleasure of spinning. It's not a moral judgment, and often fibers I don't like to *spin myself* are perfectly good fibers, but like every spinner, I have e.g. a "natural" treadling speed (fast, in my case) and that affects what's "easier" or "harder" or "more pleasant" for me to spin.

My personal bias is toward silk, cotton, ramie, as fibers whose behavior I have a slightly better understanding of from other arts (hand sewing, embroidery, cross-stitch). I tend to struggle with *wool* as even during my brief flirtation with knitting (sorry, I hated doing it!), I defaulted to *cotton* yarn.

# Fibers, unblended
Rated from 1 (ugh, I hate working with this) to 5 (BETTER THAN UNMENTIONABLE THINGS).

- **alpaca: huacaya**: 4
- **bamboo**: 2
  - I find this a PITA to spin.
- **catten floof**: 3 (yes, my catten)
  - Not that difficult but the shorter (~1") staple length requires more attention and it doesn't have a ton of "give" before it snaps.
- **cotton**: 4
  - Fast speed, slow uptake, spin fine.
  - This is a *learning curve* but I enjoy it! Probably the most difficult to spin as it requires attentiveness.
  - That said, everyone who says it's not *possible* to spin this without a faster wheel (or a charkha) is wrong. I can spin this at a 6:1 ratio on an Ashford Traveller. So empirically, one does not *need* a 40:1 accelerated wheel, although I bet it's more *pleasant* and I personally lust after a Very Fancy Wheel someday.
- **flax: tow**: 3
  - I wet-spun this when I tried some. An easy spin, but it's better for cordage than anything I'd have against my skin.
- **lotus silk**: 5
  - Fast-ish speed, slow uptake, spin fine.
  - BETTER THAN UNMENTIONABLE. Bast fiber, but with a texture that resembles mulberry silk. Incredibly fine and beautiful. I'm looking forward to finding how it *dyes*.
  - Prefers to be spun (of the wheels I currently own) on the Ashford Traditional: faster speed but slower uptake. The Louët S90 goes *much* faster but I can't yet manage the AGGRESSIVE uptake, apparently common in older Louëts.
- **ramie**: 4
  - Fast-ish speed, medium uptake, spin fine, wet spinning.
  - I wanted to try this as, like silk, I think of this (joke) as an "ancestral" fiber.
  - Not particularly difficult. More lustrous/sheeny than *tow* flax, but I haven't tried *line* flax yet.
- **silk: eri**: 4
  - Fast speed, slower uptake, spin fine. Should feel like you're "overspinning."
  - I love spinning this but the gold color is pretty distinctive, so I'd plan a project around it.
- **silk: mulberry/bombyx**: 5
  - Fast speed, slower uptake, spin fine. Should feel like you're "overspinning."
  - The Rolls-Royce of protein fibers as far as I'm concerned.
