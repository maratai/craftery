- [How to knit the Brioche Stitch for beginners](https://nimble-needles.com/stitches/how-to-knit-the-brioche-stitch/) (with video tutorials).

- [Jedao Gloves pattern by Ursula Georges](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/jedao-gloves) (free pattern on Ravelry).
- [Tutorial: a two-color cast-on](https://www.lavisch.com/site/two-color-cast-on/) (La Visch Designs). An alternative to the Italian/brioche two-color cast-on.
