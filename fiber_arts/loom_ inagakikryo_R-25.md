# Specifications (English)


The weaving machine uses the upper heddle type.

This creates more space inside than the lower heddle type, making it easier to operate the treadle.

Its depth allows for longer warp threads, enabling weaving with stronger warp tension. The standard heddle system accommodates up to six heddles, allowing for more complex weaves. Adding eight treadles to the standard six when ordering allows for up to eight heddles.

The finish is carefully polished with 1000-grit sandpaper, and the thread path is made of cherry wood.
The cherry wood from the weaving machine is processed in our own unique way to minimize stress on the warp threads.

We never use plywood, and each machine is made to order using traditional materials.

It is built to last and durable, even with years of use.

**Removable Heddle System:** The weaving machine's heddle system is removable. By removing the tsumaki, you can easily insert a chair into the loom for tasks such as threading the reed and heddle, and easily tie the heddle and treadle together. 

**Depth can be adjusted by rearranging the loom**: The H25 model can be rearranged to shorten its depth. This allows you to rearrange the loom depending on your needs , such as when you need a smaller space for your loom due to a change in location , or when you want to shorten the depth as much as possible for warp ikat weaving. 

**Assembling the loom**: Each loom is labeled with a transparent number sticker, making assembly a breeze even for first-time loom makers. Assembly instructions and tools are included. We also provide thorough assembly support via email and phone.

# Specifications (Japanese)

織前は上津巻き式。
下巻き式よりも中に空間ができ、楽に踏み木を操作できます。
奥行きがあり、経糸が長く張れるため、経糸のテンションが強い織も可能です。

綜絖は標準で6枚綜絖まで対応、ある程度複雑な組織にも対応しています。
ご注文時に踏木を標準の6本から8本に追加して頂くと8枚綜絖まで可能です。

仕上げは1000番のペーパーで丁寧に磨きをかけ、糸の通り道を桜材で製作しています。
間丁の桜材は当店独自の加工で経糸に負担がかからないよう仕上げています。

合板など一切使用せず、昔ながらの材料で一台一台、オーダーでお作りしています。
永年のご使用にも、壊れにくく、頑丈に製作しています。
