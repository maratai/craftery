# Dyeing
I'm strictly a hobbyist. I don't attempt color matching or exact colorways, and I *like* variegation. Sometimes the results are disappointing but that's the learning curve!

# Malabrigo Silky Alpaca
I bought a skein in Natural and used Dharma Procion MX fiber reactive dyes with the ["vinegar" method for silk and wool](https://www.dharmatrading.com/blogs/a/fiber-reactive-dye-dyeing-wool-silk) to get the approximate hue I wanted! I do this on the stovetop; it's very forgiving.
