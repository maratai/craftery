# Treadle Wheels

## Ashford Traditional
My second treadle wheel, bought for $100 USD off FaceBook Marketplace locally (I *pounced*). Whoever previously owned her kept her in immaculate condition. I love her. r/Handspinning ID'd her as a 1981 model. Mine's single treadle, Scotch tension; only came with the one bobbin, but I have bobbins for the Ashford Traveller, and the price was great for a wheel in excellent working condition!

## Ashford Traveller
My first treadle wheel, secondhand off eBay with a *lot* of extras (lazy kate, niddy noddy, Turkish spindle, a ton of fiber). Mine's single treadle, Scotch tension. Currently set up for plying. Technically "portable" but not in a casual stroll way, more like car transport.
