# Mystery
- Jesse Q. Sutanto. *Dial A for Aunties*. I did not think a murder mystery could be constantly, uproariously laugh-out-loud funny, but this is! I also spent this book scream-laughing in recognition; my birth family is Korean rather than South Asian but let me tell you, there's a nonzero overlap in family dynamics. I read this in audiobook from the library, A++. **Recommended**.
