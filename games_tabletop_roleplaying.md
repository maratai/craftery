# Tabletop Roleplaying Games

- *Dialect: A Game About Language and How It Dies*.
- *The Epic of Dreams: Basilisk Edition*. A rare example of modulo arithmetic in game mechanics. I have not played it but I'm intrigued.
- *Gun&Slinger*. This touts itself as "separat[ing] the Western genre from it's [sic] long history celebrating ideals of genocide and colonialism" (11), in particular calling out the genre's historical dependence on "The Other," while displacing the allegedly deprecated "The Other" to a fictive It's Always Malevolent Evil force ("The Twist") with no apparent sense of irony. Some interesting ideas; but you know, "We decided to go in on Western [genre] roleplaying" is fine without logically inconsistent ethical tap-dancing. (I say this as someone most of whose TTRPG experience is *Pathfinder* of "murder hobo" fame since that's one of the games popular in my area.)
- *The Lost and the Jammed: High lethality gungeoncrawling*. Interesting revolver-inspired (graphically, anyway) "dice chain" mechanic in a science fantasy postapocalyptic gunslinger setting.
